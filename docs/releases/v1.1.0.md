**Rabbit Ears 1.1** — show rotation, commercials and a DVR mode, from feedback on r/MiyooMini.

**Show rotation.** Put show folders inside a channel folder (`Cartoons/Doug/Season 1/…`, `Cartoons/Rugrats/…`) and the channel airs one episode of each show in turn, in episode order — no renaming files. SETUP lets you pick *rotate shows*, *in order* or *shuffle*, and an `order.txt` in a channel folder sets one channel differently.

**Commercials.** Old commercials in `Media/TV/Commercials` play before each show and in breaks during it. A folder in there named after a channel is just for that channel (G4 gets the game ads). Turn them on or off, set how often breaks come and how many ads play, all from SETUP. Breaks are part of the schedule, so the guide's times stay right.

**DVR mode.** SETUP → MODE → DVR: browse channel → show → season → episode and watch on demand. It remembers where you stopped, plays the next episode when one ends, and marks what you've watched.

**Also**
- START now opens SETUP; SELECT is the channel guide
- Bigger text in the channel guide
- On the Mini V4 (750×560) the screens are scaled to fill the panel, so text is no longer tiny
- A (display) during a break shows how long until the show comes back
- **vhsify:** fixed missing dialogue (or no sound at all) when converting shows with 5.1 surround audio. It mixed only the front left and right speakers to mono and dropped the centre channel, where the dialogue is. Re-convert affected shows with `--force`.
